# Privacy Policy for GoStopIyagi

**Last updated: October 21, 2026**

## Overview
GoStopIyagi is a Go-Stop (Hwatu) card game.

## Data Collection
GoStopIyagi does **not** collect, store, or transmit any personal data to the developer. Game data stays on your own device. It has no accounts, no analytics, and no advertising.

## Network Use
LAN play connects directly to the other player's device on your local network; no developer server is involved.
