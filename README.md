# GoStopIyagi

**Korean Hwatu (Hanafuda) card games on your PC — Go-Stop for 2–3 players and Sambong. Play a sharp AI or friends over LAN.**

[English](README.md) · [한국어](README_ko.md)

## Why GoStopIyagi?

- **An AI that thinks ahead.** The computer opponent plans its moves (MCTS), so even solo games stay tense.
- **LAN play with friends.** One player hosts, the others join on the same network — three-player Go-Stop included.
- **Real rules, scored right.** Brights, animals, ribbons and junk, combos, Go or Stop, and hand bonuses like shaking — all counted correctly.
- **More than Go-Stop.** Also includes **Sambong**, a separate game of scoring "peak" cards.
- **A table with a voice.** A male or female caller shouts "Go!", "Ppeok!", "Ttadak!" and every big moment.
